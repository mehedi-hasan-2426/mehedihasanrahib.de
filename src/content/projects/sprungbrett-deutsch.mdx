## Why

Learners preparing for A2 need short daily practice with instant feedback, not a pile of PDFs.

Originally called Sprungbrett Deutsch, the project is now Sprichio at [sprichio.de](https://sprichio.de).

## What I built

- 12 weekly units with vocabulary, grammar, reading, speaking, writing and quizzes
- 35 grammar topics with explanations, examples and graded practice
- A public introduction with a sample exercise before entering the course
- Google sign-in for approved accounts plus a guest mode that keeps progress in the browser
- Progress sync to Postgres with session ownership, origin checks and versioned saves
- Flashcards with spaced repetition and a writing area with model texts and self-assessment

Guest progress stays in the browser and can be exported as a backup. It is never automatically uploaded to an
account. Google sign-in is limited to approved accounts. Listening practice is not finished yet.

## How it is checked

- Unit and content tests
- Playwright tests on desktop and phone
- Strict CSP checked on every build
- CodeQL and secret scanning in CI
